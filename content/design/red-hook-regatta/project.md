In 2026 created a scale replica of the NYC Ferry to compete in the Red Hook Regatta.

I created the model in OnShape using reference drawings of the ferry.  She was 3D printed in multiple pieces because of the size then painted and fitted with electronics.

After my favorite ferry in the NYC fleet, she was named Ferry Godmother.
